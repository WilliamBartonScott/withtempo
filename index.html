How it works

  • Phase lengths are in beats. At 60 BPM, 1 beat = 1 second.
  • Each rep runs down, hold, up, hold. Tap Down or Up to choose which one it starts on.
  • Rep and cue calls play between beats.
  • Pausing restarts the current rep after a 3-2-1.
  • After a set, the stopwatch times your rest. Tap it to clear.
  • The metronome stops when you close it and uses the BPM from settings.
  • Turn off silent mode so beats play on iPhone.
  • For more voices: Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices.
